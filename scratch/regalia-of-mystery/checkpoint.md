**Armor penetration: SOLVED (2026-11-24).** An Upgrade-slot mod carrying our keyword `MoM_Blade_KW` and our own enchantment (ENCH -> MGEF Add Perk -> our PERK, copied from the vanilla chain) delivers the perk on equip with no console, script or holotape. The perk is a copy of the base `PlayerPerk` melee entry, weapon-tab `HasKeyword`, multiplier 0.70 (30%). Eye of Ra is a worn item with a keyword plus a second perk entry (multiplier 0.0, attacker-tab `WornHasKeyword`); multipliers stack, so Blade alone is 30% and Blade plus Eye is 100%. All verified in game on the SKK dummy. Details: spec "Armor penetration: in-game findings" (rounds 2 and 3). Built by `build/xedit/BuildBladePerk2.pas` (plugin `RegaliaPerkTest3`). The weapon-record approach (`BuildBlade.pas`) stays superseded.

**State (2026-11-24, end of the long session):** the Blade works as its own weapon (`build/xedit/BuildBladeWeapon.pas`, test plugin `RegaliaWeapon1`): named exactly "Blade of Bastet", Chemistry Station recipe, damage x2.2 (70 at Strength 10), weight 2, VATS cost about 20, bleed (unchecked in game), 30% armor penetration through our own enchantment -> effect -> perk chain, and the Eye of Ra (a worn keyword plus a second perk entry) takes it to 100%. Medium swing speed by decision (the Speed property does nothing for melee). All verified on the SKK dummy. Details in the spec, rounds 2 to 6.
